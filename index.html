KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Spot the scam before it starts.

Fraudsters often create fear, excitement or urgency. Slow down and check first.

🔑

SECRET INFORMATION

OTP & PIN scams

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, password or ATM PIN—even with someone claiming to be from a bank.

01
💸

DIGITAL PAYMENTS

UPI & QR tricks

You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Check the name and amount before paying.

02
🔗

UNKNOWN LINKS

Fake links & messages

Avoid suspicious links promising prizes, urgent KYC updates, free gifts or instant benefits.

03
🧾

OFFERS & IDENTITY

Fake jobs & schemes

Verify job offers, subsidies and government schemes through official websites or offices. Never pay a stranger to “release” a benefit.

04

YOUR EVERYDAY DIGITAL SHIELD

Five habits. Better protection.

You don't need to be a technology expert. Start with these small habits and share them with your family.

01
Use a strong screen lock

Set a PIN or password that others cannot easily guess.

02
Verify before you trust

Call the person or organisation using a known, official number.

03
Download safely

Install apps from trusted app stores; avoid unknown APK files.

04
Keep your phone updated

Install updates and review app permissions.

05
Tell someone you trust

Ask a trusted family member or bank branch when a message feels suspicious.
